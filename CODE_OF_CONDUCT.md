# Satin Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Participants should be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good
intent while discussing ideas, focus criticism on the work, welcome different
levels of experience, and protect other people's privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to Satin's issue tracker, pull requests, release
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Send a private report
through the contact path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and prefix the title with
`Conduct report`. The maintainer will review the report promptly, protect the
reporter's privacy, and may edit or remove content, close discussions, or block
participants when necessary. Reports involving the maintainer may instead be
submitted through GitHub's own abuse-reporting process.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and
the safety of affected participants. Retaliation against a reporter or witness
is itself a violation of this policy.
